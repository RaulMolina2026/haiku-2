Lighting One Candle

"The light of a candle
Is transferred to another candle.
Spring twilight"

- By Yosa Buson -

Haikus focus on a brief moment in time, juxtaposing two images, and creating a sudden sense of enlightenment.
A good example of this is haiku master Yosa Busons comparison of a singular candle with the starry wonderment of the spring sky.
